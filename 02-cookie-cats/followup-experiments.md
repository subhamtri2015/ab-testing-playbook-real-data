# Follow-up experiments

1. Early gate vs later gate with an explicit reward
   - Hypothesis: clear reward framing may offset the retention risk.
   - Primary metric: 7-day retention.
   - Guardrail: total rounds and churn.
   - Sample size: use the observed D7 baseline and the pre-specified decision MDE.

2. Gate placement with a reduced wait time
   - Hypothesis: a softer throttle may improve engagement without harming the first-session experience.
   - Primary metric: 7-day retention.
   - Guardrail: satisfaction or in-app feedback.

3. Segment by first-session depth
   - Hypothesis: new players who reach the end of level 30 may be more sensitive to the gate move.
   - Primary metric: D7 retention among the eligible segment.
   - Guardrail: game rounds and player count.
