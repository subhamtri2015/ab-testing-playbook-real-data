# Segmentation and exposure

The share of players reaching the gate is lower in gate_40 than gate_30. This is important because the treatment only works if users actually experience it. In this dataset, the exposure rate is around 18.5% vs 15.3%.

The subgroup of players with rounds >= 40 is presented as exploratory because it is defined after randomization. The group-level retention is 50.29% vs 48.51%, which is suggestive but not a basis for a causal claim. This pattern is a classic warning sign for selected subgroup effects and should not be used as a stand-alone launch argument.
