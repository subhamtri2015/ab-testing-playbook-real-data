# Metric definitions

## Retention

Retention is the share of users who return after a set period. In this repo, D1 retention and D7 retention are measured as binary indicators.

## CTR vs click-through probability

CTR is a ratio of clicks divided by impressions. Click-through probability is the implied probability of a click for a single user or exposure. In this dataset, CTR is the primary aggregate metric for the Upworthy archive.

## Conversion

A conversion rate is a share of users or exposures that complete a desired event. The general formula is:

conversion = successful events / eligible population

where the eligible population depends on the product action and the measurement window.
